Project detail

Agro-ecology for improved nutrition and income for rural poor farmers.

This project illustrates SURUDEV’s broader mission: strengthening rural livelihoods while restoring ecosystems and improving local resilience in the Njising-Tabenken Montane forest region.

Project overview

Turning local agriculture into a resilient, productive system.

The project focuses on improving nutrition and income for poor rural farmers by helping them move toward agro-ecological systems and more sustainable practices.

By linking environmental stewardship with food production, SURUDEV supports both ecosystem health and household wellbeing.

At a glance

  • Location: Njising-Tabenken Montane forest
  • Focus: Agroecology, nutrition, livelihoods
  • Approach: Community-centered and field-based

Challenge

Smallholder communities need practical ways to improve production without degrading natural systems.

Rural communities often face environmental pressure, food insecurity, and limited opportunity to adopt resilient agricultural systems. SURUDEV’s project addresses this by combining ecological restoration with local knowledge and practical operations.

“The goal is to make sustainable agriculture a realistic path to both food security and environmental recovery.”

Approach

Practical action on small farms and shared landscapes.

Local knowledge

Building on community experience and practices that align with environmental resilience.

Soil and crop health

Promoting methods that support nutrition, productivity, and longer-term landscape stability.

Community learning

Demonstration and training help farmers apply approaches in ways that fit their realities.
