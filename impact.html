Environmental results that are practical, collective, and long-term.

Impact at a glance

SURUDEV works where environmental health and human wellbeing are inseparable.

Its projects and initiatives support biodiversity conservation, agricultural resilience, environmental education, and community adaptation. Each is designed to help ecosystems recover without leaving local people behind.

Key focus areas

  • Biodiversity and habitat protection
  • Forest and landscape restoration
  • Sustainable agriculture and food security
  • Climate resilience and adaptation
  • Community livelihood development
  • Research, education, and advocacy

Where the work happens

Field-based action across Cameroon’s landscapes.

Campo Ma’an National Park

One of the biodiversity hotspots referenced by the organization, important for wildlife and ecological monitoring.

Njising-Tabenken Montane forest

Associated with agroecology and community livelihoods initiatives to improve nutrition and income.

Kom-Wum forest

Connected with conservation work focused on chimpanzee conservation and local ecological awareness.
