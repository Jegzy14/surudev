Field note

Restoring biodiversity through community stewardship.

In landscapes under pressure, SURODEV’s work shows that communities can protect natural systems while strengthening the opportunities they depend on.

Context

Local action is central to meaningful environmental recovery.

Forest restoration and biodiversity protection work best when communities see clear value in the process and are part of the long-term plan. This is where SURUDEV’s approach begins: with people, landscape, and practical stewardship.

Whether through conservation efforts, sustainable agriculture, or environmental education, the organization works to strengthen the systems that support both ecosystems and livelihoods.

“The work is not only about preserving nature. It is about helping communities live with it, depend on it, and protect it well.”

In practice

Environmental protection that is rooted in daily life.

Research

Understanding species, habitats, and local ecological realities helps prioritize action.

Training

Communities gain practical knowledge for restoring landscapes and improving resilience.

Action

SUSTAINABLE practices are carried into farms, restoration initiatives, and everyday systems.
