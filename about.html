About SURUDEV

Protecting biodiversity while strengthening the communities around it.

SURUDEV is a Cameroonian non-profit conservation organization founded in 2007 and legally registered in 2010. Its work brings environmental protection, research, sustainable agriculture, and local livelihoods into one practical framework.

Mission

To promote inclusive and sustainable development initiatives in Cameroon.

The organization’s mission is to address ecological degradation and poverty together—restoring landscapes, supporting communities, and building practical solutions to everyday environmental challenges.

“To address and reduce ecological degradation through conservation of biodiversity, waste management, promotion of renewable energy, and the enhancement of local climate adaptation methods.”

Vision

Communities that live well with nature.

What we envision

SURUDEV envisions sustainable development where communities are aware of environmental issues and have access to practical opportunities for food, livelihood, education, health, peace, and dignity.

Our approach

Its work integrates conservation, local knowledge, research, and community action—bringing environmental solutions closer to the realities people face every day.

Our focus

From biodiversity protection and climate adaptation to sustainable agriculture and education, the organization aims to strengthen both ecosystems and livelihoods.

Values

People, stewardship, and practical action.

Inclusive development

SURUDEV is committed to making environmental progress relevant to everyone, including vulnerable and underrepresented groups.

Knowledge and research

Research, awareness, and evidence-informed action are central to protecting ecosystems and strengthening local adaptation.

Environmental justice

Its work recognizes the relationship between ecological health, food systems, and community well-being.

Resilience through action

The organization promotes sustainable agriculture, cleaner systems, and better local governance of natural resources.

Timeline

A growing organization with clear momentum.

2007

SURUDEV was founded as a Cameroonian conservation and development organization.

2010

It was legally registered in conformity with the laws of the Republic of Cameroon.

Today

The organization continues to work across biodiversity, livelihoods, education, and humanitarian response.
