What we do

Environmental action that is grounded in local realities.

SURUDEV addresses ecosystem degradation and poverty together. Its work helps people and landscapes adapt, recover, and thrive through conservation, restoration, education, and sustainable local enterprise.

Biodiversity

Conservation of wildlife and habitats

SURUDEV promotes conservation of biodiversity and ecosystem stewardship, including work on threatened species and forest habitats.

Its field work supports monitoring, species protection, and community awareness around wildlife and natural capital.

Restoration

Forest and landscape restoration

At the center of the organization’s approach is restoration of degraded ecosystems; this includes reforestation, landscape repair, and practical local stewardship.

The work aims to build healthier environments while supporting the people who depend on them.

Agriculture

Sustainable agriculture

SURUDEV advances agroecology and sustainable agricultural practices to improve food security, nutrition, and rural incomes.

This includes promoting local seed varieties, ecological methods, and resilient farming systems.

Climate

Climate resilience

The organization supports climate adaptation through awareness, research, and locally grounded resilience practices.

This strengthens communities’ ability to cope with environmental shocks and long-term ecological pressure.

Livelihoods

Community livelihoods

SURUDEV connects conservation with income generation, alternative livelihoods, and community enterprises.

This creates practical benefits for communities while reducing pressure on natural resources.

Education

Environmental education

Education is a core pillar of SURUDEV’s work, helping communities understand environmental issues and participate in solutions.

The organization works across youth, families, and communities to build knowledge and action.

How it works

Research, community engagement, and long-term stewardship.

SURUDEV blends fieldwork, environmental education, and research to address the root causes of ecological degradation. Its work is not limited to awareness alone—it emphasizes practical implementation and community participation.

  • Community partnerships and local action
  • Research on species, resources, and adaptation
  • Education and awareness for resilience
“Our approach is rooted in inclusive, sustainable development—not through short-term interventions alone, but through lasting local systems.”
